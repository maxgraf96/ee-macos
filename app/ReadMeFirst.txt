Empire Earth for Mac - Read Me First

This app plays your own copy of Empire Earth Gold from GOG on a Mac with
Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later. It contains none of the game.

1. Install: drag EmpireEarth onto the Applications folder in this window.
   Then open it from Applications, not from here.

2. The first time you open it, macOS says "EmpireEarth" Not Opened. Click Done.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway
   next to "EmpireEarth" was blocked. Enter your password, then click Open
   Anyway again. You only do this once.

3. When macOS asks whether EmpireEarth may find devices on your local network,
   click Allow. Multiplayer needs it.

4. The app asks for your game. Choose GOG's offline installer,
   setup_empire_earth_gold_....exe (gog.com > Account > Games > Empire Earth
   Gold > Download offline backup game installers > Windows), or an installed
   "Empire Earth Gold" folder. It then builds the game for your Mac, once:
   about 2 to 5 minutes. After that it starts straight into the game.

5. Your files are in ~/Library/Application Support/EmpireEarth (in Finder:
   Go > Go to Folder...). To remove everything, quit the game, then move
   EmpireEarth from Applications and that folder to the Bin. The folder holds
   your saved games.

6. Multiplayer: use the game list (Multiplayer > Other Multiplayer), with
   everyone on the same network or on ZeroTier. Direct Connect does not work
   with GOG keys.

7. If something goes wrong, send these two files from
   ~/Library/Application Support/EmpireEarth/Logs: game.log and launcher.log.
